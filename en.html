Tapsis

Every phone has a creature of its own, a Tapsi. Children meet, schnupp each other’s Tapsi, and their own slowly grows into a legend.

For parents in brief

  • No account, no server, no ads, no analytics. The Tapsi is kept on the device only.
  • Schnupping works without the internet: one phone shows its stars, the other reads them with the camera.
  • Every day there is one free Schnupp. Tapsis can be played in full without buying anything.
  • Purchases are only in the grown-ups’ corner, behind a parental gate.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get to the grown-ups’ corner?

On the start screen, below the big “Schnupp” button, it says “For grown-ups” in small letters. Four numbers then appear as words, such as “seven, three, zero, nine”; type them in as digits. The numbers are new every time.

The egg does not hatch.

The egg takes ten minutes, even with the app closed. Once the time is up, your child taps the egg three times. If you don’t want to wait: at the very bottom it says “For grown-ups: hatch it now”.

The stars are not read.

Hold the two phones about an arm’s length apart and turn up the brightness of the phone showing the stars. Stars only count if the clocks of both phones agree: switch on “Set automatically” for date and time in the settings. If the app has no camera access, allow it in the device settings under Tapsis.

The same Tapsi cannot be schnupped again.

Each Tapsi can be schnupped once a day. Tomorrow it works again.

How do purchases get to a new phone?

Tap “Restore purchases” in the grown-ups’ corner. Bought costumes and the Schnupp machine come back if the phone is signed in with the same store account.

How does the Tapsi get to a new phone?

With the device backup. Tapsis has no account and no cloud of its own. Without a backup, and when switching to a phone with a different operating system, the Tapsi is gone, along with any Schnupps saved up.

What does the app do with camera and microphone?

The camera only reads stars. When singing, the microphone only measures how loud it is, and it only switches on when your child presses a button for it. No picture and no sound is saved or sent. More in the privacy policy.

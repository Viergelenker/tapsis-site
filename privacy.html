Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: Tapsis is an app for children. It has no account, no server, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. The Tapsi and everything that belongs to it is kept on the device only. Camera and microphone are processed on the device; no picture and no sound is saved or sent. The developer receives no data. Purchases are handled by Apple and Google. On Android, two components from Google send technical diagnostic data to Google (section 8).

2. The app

In Tapsis every phone has a creature of its own, a Tapsi. Children meet, read the other Tapsi’s stars with the camera (“schnupping”), and their own Tapsi grows from it. Tapsis is made for children and follows the children’s rules of Apple and Google. Purchases and anything that leads outside the app are kept behind a parental gate in the grown-ups’ corner.

3. What is stored on the device

So that the Tapsi is still there next time, the app stores on the device:

  • the child’s own Tapsi: a random number its looks are made from, a name the child picked from three suggestions, its stage, stardust and mood,
  • the friend book: name, stage and costume of the Tapsis the child has schnupped, how often and on which day they last met,
  • costumes, finds and records from the flying game Zoidern,
  • how many Schnupps are left for today and saved up,
  • the numbers of purchases already delivered, so that no purchase counts twice.

The app never asks for the child’s name, age or anything else about the child, and it has no field for free text. None of this is sent to the developer or to third parties.

4. Schnupping: from screen to camera

To be schnupped, a phone shows stars on its screen. They only say things about the Tapsi: its random number, the number of its name, its stage, mood and costume, the time window the stars were made in, and a code that proves they are genuine. Another phone reads them with its camera and puts the Tapsi into its friend book. Afterwards it can show thank-you stars that tell the first phone about it. All of this happens directly between the two devices, without the internet and without a server. Nothing about the child is in it.

5. Camera

The camera is the magic lens: it looks for stars in the camera picture. On devices that support augmented reality, the schnupped Tapsi then hops into the room; for this the camera picture and the motion sensors are processed on the device (with Apple’s ARKit on iPhone and iPad, with ARCore on Android, see section 8). The app neither saves nor sends any pictures. Camera access can be refused, or withdrawn later in the device settings; then only schnupping does not work.

6. Microphone

When singing, the Tapsi listens and sings along. The microphone is only switched on when the child presses the button for it, and while it is on, “Microphone on” is shown at the top. The app turns the sound into a number for its loudness at once and throws the sound away. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent. Leaving the screen or the app switches the microphone off.

7. Motion sensors and vibration

When dancing and while the egg is hatching, the Tapsi feels how the device is moved. The readings are only processed on the device at that moment; they are neither saved nor sent. Vibrations are made by the device itself and create no data.

8. Android only: components from Google

On iPhone and iPad, Tapsis only uses features that Apple runs on the device to read stars and to place the Tapsi in the room. On Android these features come from Google:

ML Kit (reading the stars)

On Android the stars are read by ML Kit, a library from Google that works on the device. The camera pictures stay on the device. According to Google, however, ML Kit sends technical data for diagnostics and usage statistics to Google: information about the device (manufacturer, model, Android version) and the app (package name, version), a per-installation identifier that according to Google is not intended to identify a person or a device, and performance data such as latency, image size and error codes. It is sent encrypted (HTTPS); according to Google, ML Kit does not pass this data on to third parties. The developer receives none of it. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: the legitimate interest in reading stars reliably. More: developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Tapsis only uses Google Play Services for AR if they are already installed on the phone; otherwise the simple magic lens is used. The app does not install them.

9. Purchases

Tapsis can be played in full without buying anything. In the grown-ups’ corner, behind the parental gate, parents can buy Schnupps, costumes and the Schnupp machine. The purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play. The developer learns neither payment details nor account details. The app checks the store’s receipt on the device and keeps the number of the purchase (section 3). The privacy policies of Apple and Google apply to the purchase.

10. No collection by the developer

The developer does not collect, store or process any personal data of users. In Tapsis there is:

  • no sign-up and no account,
  • no server of the developer that data could go to,
  • no advertising, no advertising IDs, no tracking,
  • no analytics or statistics of the developer,
  • no chat, no free text input, no location,
  • no links out of the app for children (the links to this site are in the grown-ups’ corner, behind the parental gate).

11. Backups and new phones

Depending on the device settings, the Tapsi is included in the device backup: on iPhone and iPad in iCloud or on a computer, on Android in Google’s backup. The privacy policies of Apple and Google apply. Tapsis has no cloud of its own. Without a backup, and when switching to a device with a different operating system, the Tapsi is lost; bought costumes and the Schnupp machine can be restored.

12. Deletion

Deleting the app deletes the Tapsi and everything that belongs to it. Purchases stay on record with Apple or Google.

13. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). As the developer holds no personal data, there is nothing to disclose or erase; it does not exist there. For the data ML Kit sends to Google on Android, Google is the right contact. You are welcome to write to the email address above at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

14. Children

Tapsis is made for children. That is why the app asks nothing about the child, shows no ads, contains no analytics and does not lead children out of the app. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

15. Contact by email

If you email the developer, your address and the content of the message are processed to answer you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The message is deleted once your request is settled and no legal retention period applies.

16. This website

This website sets no cookies and contains no analytics, advertising or social media plug-ins. When it is accessed, the hosting provider processes technically necessary access data, such as IP address, time, page requested and browser type, to deliver the site and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The operator of this website does not analyse this data.

17. Changes

If what the app does changes, this policy is updated. The version published on this page applies. A German version is available as Datenschutzerklärung; the German version is authoritative.
